## Premium purchases

Agitation is free to download. Core features require Agitation Premium, available through an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription, an eligible free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Premium unlocks the complete film development timer, film and developer data lookup, record history, sample-photo attachments, and advanced record management features.

Monthly and yearly subscription pricing, duration, free-trial eligibility, renewal, and cancellation are managed by Apple through your App Store account. A free trial is usually available once per subscription group. Unless canceled before the trial ends, the selected subscription renews automatically.

The lifetime option is a one-time purchase that unlocks Premium without renewal. You can restore purchases in Agitation, and you can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.
